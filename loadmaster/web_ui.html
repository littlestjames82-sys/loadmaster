Check a load

Take a photo of the loaded trailer. A side view with the wheels in frame works best; use a close-up of each tie-down anchor when you want tie-downs counted.

Verdicts use every view together: the side view carries the measurements, the rear view judges lights and plate, close-ups carry the tie-down count. Corrections work on the main view.

Bed length turns pixels into inches. Load length turns on the rule verdicts. Both can be left blank, and the app will say what it could not check.
